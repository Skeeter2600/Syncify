📋 How to get your ARL token manually:

  1. Log in to deezer.com in your browser.
  2. Press F12 (or right-click → Inspect) to open Developer Tools.
  3. Go to the Application tab (Chrome/Edge/Brave) or Storage tab (Firefox).
  4. Expand Cookies on the left and select https://www.deezer.com.
  5. Find the cookie named arl, copy its value, and paste it below.

💡 Note: Deezer cookies are HttpOnly, meaning automated bookmarklets or page scripts are blocked by the browser for security. Copying manually is the only reliable way.
